UP Police Sub Inspector SI Recruitment 2025 : Vacancy Details ➜

Post Name Eligibility Criteria
UP Police Sub Inspector (SI)
  • Candidates Must Hold A Graduation (Bachelor’s) Degree From A Recognized University Or Institution In India Or An Equivalent Qualification Recognized By The Government.
  • It Is Mandatory To Upload The Graduation Mark Sheet.
  • If The Graduation Degree Certificate Is Not Available, Then The Provisional Graduation Degree Must Be Uploaded.
  • If The Provisional Graduation Degree Is Also Not Available, Candidates Should Upload Their Mark Sheets In Its Place.
  • During Document Verification And Physical Standard Test, Candidates Must Produce The Original Graduation Degree Certificate Along With The Graduation Mark Sheets.

Important Points
  • Passing The O Level Computer Examination Conducted By Nielit (Formerly Doeacc) Or An Equivalent Certification.
  • Minimum Two Years Service In The Territorial Army.
  • NCC B Certificate.
  • Read The Official Notification Completely Before Applying.
