Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) has invited application for the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) September 2026 examination. The online application process for CTET September Recruitment 2026 has started on 11 May 2026, and candidates can apply till 10 June 2026. Candidates must check the complete details for CTET September Recruitment 2026, which are given below

CTET September Notification 2026 : Age Limit ➜

Minimum : 18 Years

CTET September Online Form 2026 : Educational Qualification ➜

Class Eligibility
Primary Stage (Class I To V) Candidates Who Are Senior Secondary Or Its Equivalent With At Least 50% Or 45% Marks/Passed Or Appearing Diploma In Elementary Education As Per NCTE 2002 Norms / B.El.Ed(04 Years Course)/Diploma In Education(Special Course ,For 02 Years)/ D.Ed(1 Year)/Diploma In Elementary Education(1 Year) Will Be Eligible For CTET Examination
Secondary Stage (Class VI To VIII) Candidates Having Bachelor Degree (With At Least 45% Or 50% Marks) /Passed Or Appearing In Final Year Of 2 Year Diploma In Elementary Education/Diploma In Education/B.El.Ed /B.SC.Ed /B.A Ed. /B.Ed. Will Be Eligible For This CTET Examination.

CTET September Online Form 2026 : Selection Process ➜

Selection Will Be Based On Entrance Exam (Paper I & Paper II)
