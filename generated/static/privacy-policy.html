Overview

Sarkari Suchna India (SarkariSuchnaIndia.com) publishes government job notifications, results, admit cards and related exam updates for informational purposes. We aim to collect only the minimum data needed to operate the website securely and improve user experience.

Note: We are an information portal, not a government recruitment authority. Official applications are completed only on respective board or commission websites.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Contact details you voluntarily provide when emailing us for support, corrections or feedback.
  • Usage data such as pages visited, referral source, device type and general interaction patterns.
  • Technical data including browser type, IP address (often in aggregated or truncated form), and error logs used for security and performance.
  • Cookie data as described in the Cookies & analytics section below.

We do not require account registration to browse public job listings on this website.

How We Use Information

We use collected information for legitimate purposes related to running and improving the service:

  • To maintain website availability, security and performance.
  • To understand which content categories are most useful to visitors.
  • To respond to support requests, correction reports and user feedback.
  • To detect abuse, spam or automated traffic that may harm service quality.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations when required.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and measure traffic trends. For example, analytics tools may help us understand how visitors discover and use pages.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features, but core public job browsing should remain available.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data only when necessary:

  • With service providers that help us host, secure or analyze the website, under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order or to protect the rights and safety of users and the platform.
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger or restructuring, with continued protection of user data.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below and include enough information for us to verify your request.

We may retain certain records where required for security, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, email us at support@sarkarisuchnaindia.com with the subject line Privacy Request.
