Email

For all inquiries, write to:

support@sarkarisuchnaindia.com

Please use a clear subject line such as Correction Request, Broken Link, or General Support so we can route your message faster.

Support Hours

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM (IST)

Messages received outside these hours are reviewed on the next working day.

Before You Write

To help us resolve your request quickly, include as much detail as possible:

  1. The full page URL where you found the issue.
  2. A screenshot or copied text showing the problem.
  3. For recruitment queries: exam/board name, post title and notification date.
  4. For corrections: a link or reference to the official notification.

What We Cannot Do

Sarkari Suchna India is an information website. We cannot accept job applications, issue admit cards, publish results on behalf of any board, or influence recruitment decisions.

For application status, fees or exam centre issues, please contact the official recruiting authority directly.
